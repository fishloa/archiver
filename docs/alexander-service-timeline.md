# Alexander Czernin — military and work timeline, 1939–1947

Compiled 14 October 2026 from documents already in the archive. Every line names its source. **Dates are as the
documents give them; where two sources differ both are shown.** Unit descriptions in the third column are the
compiler's general understanding of the German designations, not statements in his papers.

Sources: **TH** = TH Wien Personalakt (record 3505, page numbers are `seq`); **WASt** = Bundesarchiv WASt
cover letter and cards B 563-1 KARTEI C-30/456 and C-179/493 (record 3500, pages 1 and 3);
**Gauakt** = Gauakt 232.006 (records 3499, 4024); **Meld.** = Vienna Meldeunterlagen (record 3504, and the
Meldekarte quoted in the research log); **NS14** = BArch NS 14/165, Alexander's pages (record 4060);
**R26** = Reichsforschungsrat Forscherkarte (record 4057); **Log** = `docs/research-log.md`.

## After 31 December 1943

**Nothing in any document we hold.** The Arbeitsurlaub ran to 31 Dec 1943; what followed is not recorded. Later
points, none from his own papers: 6 May 1946, Vienna deregistration to Alt-Aussee (a date to reconcile, see Log);
British custody at Braunschweig, January 1947 (Log, unverified); the family account (Stephanie Czernin,
1 Nov 2025): jaundice, released for ill health, wanted for a friend's factory, "never said he deserted".

## Contradictions and open questions

1. **Call-up:** 10 Feb 1940 (TH: Einberufungsbefehl of 6 Feb) against 12 Feb 1940 (Meldezettel, SD report).
2. **Two Feldpostnummern:** 18632 (Jan 1941) and 17 578 (Feb 1942); neither has been matched to a unit.
3. **The WASt letter lists only Army units**; the Luftwaffe transfer of 25 Nov 1942 and its replacement unit are not
   on it, nor is any 1943–45 entry. Asked for in the supplement of 13 Oct 2026 (Zimbra 470287).
4. **Hospital:** the Krankenbuchlager found nothing (16 Jan 2026) although both cards record Schwiebus; a re-check
   went 13 Oct 2026 (470293).
5. **WNF bombing:** not in our records. From general knowledge, the works were bombed from 13 Aug 1943 with further
   raids in autumn 1943 and 1944; **unverified**, to be checked against USAAF records. He was on the site in 1943.
6. **How the Arbeitsurlaub ended**, and whether he returned to the Luftwaffe unit, is unknown.

**Handling note.** The NSBDT application (March 1940) is an adverse fact: an application to an NS-affiliated
association, though apparently made when the NSBDT called on listed Ostmark VDI members to submit applications
(13 Nov 1939 letter, record 4059 p.550, about another person). It is not to be volunteered to an Austrian body.

## Glossary of terms used above

- **Ersatz-Abteilung / Ersatz-Bataillon:** "replacement" unit: the home depot that trains recruits and holds soldiers between postings.
- **Kanonier / Gefreiter:** gunner (a private) / lance corporal.
- **Seilbahn(-Kommando), Seilbahnpionier:** aerial ropeway (cable-car) unit; an engineer-soldier trained to build and run one.
- **Eisenbahn-Bau-Bataillon:** railway-construction battalion.
- **Pionier-Bataillon / Stab / Kolonne:** combat-engineer battalion / its headquarters staff / a transport or supply column.
- **Kraftwagen-Werkstatt-Kompanie:** motor-vehicle workshop company (repairs lorries and cars).
- **Truppen-Ingenieur:** an engineer attached to a unit as a technical specialist.
- **Technischer Kriegsverwaltungsrat:** a wartime official in the military administration, technical branch; not a combat officer.
- **Kriegsbesoldung:** war pay (a soldier's or military official's pay), which replaced his university salary.
- **UK-Stellung (unabkömmlich):** a declaration that a man is indispensable at home, exempting him from call-up or service.
- **Arbeitsurlaub / Arbeitsurlauber:** work leave / a soldier lent to a firm for war production.
- **Feldpostnummer:** the field-post number that identifies a unit for mail; it can be matched to a unit.
- **NSBDT / VDI / Zentralkartei:** Nazi technical association / the main engineers' society, run within it / the NSBDT's central membership card file.
- **WASt:** the former Wehrmacht-Auskunftstelle, now Bundesarchiv Abt. PA, which holds soldiers' index cards.
