### Why this is needed

The letter already sent asked which members of the signatory families appear in
their databases. The Memorial cannot answer that: they hold prisoner cards and
have no list of who signed the declaration, nor the genealogies needed to tell a
signatory's brother from a namesake. That matching is our work, not theirs — we
hold the list of the 85 signatories (record 3497) and the family trees.

What they *can* answer is one narrow, mechanical question. František
Bořek-Dohalský's card carries the remark **"signatář Prohlášení české a moravské
šlechty"**. That annotation lives in a field of their database. If it can be
queried, every other card bearing it is evidence of exactly the kind this case
needs — and no one outside the Memorial can run that query.
