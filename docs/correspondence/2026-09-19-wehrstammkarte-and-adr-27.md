## A. Bundesarchiv Abteilung PA — Alexander's Wehrstammkarte

**To:** `poststelle-pa@bundesarchiv.de` — Bundesarchiv, Abteilung PA (the former Deutsche Dienststelle/WASt), Am Borsigturm 130, Berlin-Tegel. The letter points at the signed Benutzungsantrag of 16 September and offers a separate one if this matter needs it.

Prompted by Hubertus Trauttenberg, 19 September: *"One had to find his
Wehrstammkarte which might be in some archive in the country where he was living
resp. registered."* Nothing sent so far asks for Alexander's military record —
the Militärarchiv letter of 15 September (Zimbra 463859) is about Felix and the
Abwehr.

**The identifier we have.** Record 3505 page 30, Alexander's own letter of
22 February 1942, heads itself *"Dipl.Ing. Alexander Graf Czernin,
Truppen-Ingenieur, 17 578"* — which reads as a Feldpostnummer. If it is, the unit
is identifiable and the enquiry stops being a name search.

The letter asks for the record, not for a conclusion. It does not ask whether he
deserted: an archive asked a leading question answers the question, and what is
wanted is the card.

## B. ÖStA, Archiv der Republik — the Staatskanzlei's own § 27 file on Felix

**To:** `adr@oesta.gv.at`

The gap found on 19 September. The WStLA request of 18 September
(2026-1809203348262) buys the **Magistrat's** side of the § 27 proceeding. The
submission was lodged with the **Staatsamt für Inneres** (record 4006 page 7, by
Rechtsanwalt Dr Karl Trauttmansdorff) and the **Staatskanzlei** referred it to
the Magistrat on 9 August 1945 under **Zl. 13.413-2 N/1945** (page 12). That is a
different authority's file, and it should hold the submission as lodged and the
Staatsregierung's own decision, neither of which the municipal file need contain.

**Felix alone, by file number — deliberately not extended to the siblings.** A
§ 27 application exists because the applicant fell under the registration duty,
which attached to membership or candidacy in the NSDAP, SS, SA, NSKK or NSFK. So
asking an Austrian federal archive whether such files exist for six people asks,
in writing, whether those people were National Socialists. A nil proves little —
AdR holds the Staatskanzlei's side, not the municipal registration lists — while
a hit would be handed to the state that decides the application, by us, unasked.
MA 35 has no reason today to think Alexander was a member; the question is all
downside.

Felix by number adds no disclosure at all: the whole § 27 file was already
ordered from WStLA on 18 September.

Disclosure rules applied: the file number and the person, nothing about why.
