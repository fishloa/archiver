# MA 35 historian reports — points in our favour

Source: MA 35's historian reports, received through Stefan Huber on 3 November 2026.

- MA 35 – 391288-2023: report of 04.08.2023, saved as `2023-08-04-ma35-historisches-gutachten.pdf`.
- MA 35 – 387376-2026: reports of 27.05.2026, 12.08.2026, 24.09.2026 and 22.10.2026, saved as `2026-11-03-ma35-historische-gutachten.pdf`.

All five reports are by Bernhard Bardach.

## From the 2023 report, still standing after 2026

1. **Alexander's own citizenship record (STE) was checked under the ČSSR Compensation Act of 1975.**
   - That act compensated Austrian citizens whose assets in Czechoslovakia were confiscated or nationalised.
   - So it ties Alexander personally to assets in Czechoslovakia.
   - This contradicts 27.05.2026: "Ein enger Bezug dieses Familienzweigs zur Tschechoslowakei konnte nicht festgestellt werden". That finding is one of the grounds for separating him from Humprecht and Rudolf.
   - It is supported by record 3504: his Vienna registration shows him arriving from Schönhof, ČSR.
   - Use: Bohemian ties only. The confiscations were Czechoslovak and after the war, so they are not § 58c persecution in themselves.

2. **The historian attributed the move to Britain to the British government.**
   - 2023 wording: Alexander went "aufgrund von Bestrebungen der britischen Regierung".
   - It matches the collection from Braunschweig by two British officers on 14.01.1947 (FCDO record 3959).
   - By 24.09.2026 and 22.10.2026 this has become: "bekam … einen Job bei … Fairey Aviation und in diesem Zusammenhang begab er sich in das Vereinigte Königreich".
   - The 2026 reports then use it to say that persecution "mit Sicherheit nicht" caused the departure.

3. **The historian wrote in 2023 that Vertipedia "kann seitens des Historikers nicht verifiziert werden".**
   - From 24.09.2026 onward, the "kriegswichtiges Forschungsprojekt" premise rests mainly on Vertipedia, plus the Knobling chapter (Hirschel/Prem/Madelung 2004, pp. 306–308).
   - Neither source places Alexander on the project in March–April 1945.

4. **The 2023 caseworker's note records the war work as compulsion: "gezwungen den Kriegseinsatz zu unterstützen".**
   - The 2026 reports read the same work as a sign of the regime's trust.
   - The documents support compulsion. Record 3505 says he was "als Arbeitsurlauber zu den Wiener-Neustädter Flugzeugwerken kommandiert", i.e. ordered there as a soldier from 7.1.1943, and the secondment ended 31.10.1943.

## Conceded in the 2026 reports

- 27.05.2026: Humprecht and Rudolf were persecuted, "unbestritten".
- 22.10.2026: Felix was held by the Gestapo for Hoch- und Landesverrat from 28.03 to 07.04.1945 and was officially recognised as a victim.
- 22.10.2026: close relatives of political persecutees could be persecuted without any involvement of their own (§ 83(2) and § 139 RStGB; the Haas, Cupal and Coppi cases).
- 22.10.2026: it was "grundsätzlich möglich" that Alexander had grounds to fear persecution because of Felix. The only counter-argument offered is the war-project premise.

## Factual errors in the 2026 reports

- 24.09.2026 says there is no record of Alexander being registered in Austria after the war, and that he probably left "zeitnahe nach dem Krieg".
  - Record 4021 (WStLA) shows Wien III, Metternichgasse 10/4 from 20.11.1943 to 6.5.1946, then deregistered to Alt-Aussee.
  - Departure to Britain: 14.01.1947.
- 12.08.2026, footnote 1: the historian cannot read Czech, so the Czech sources were not assessed (Hazdra, Hořejš and others).
