## Note for the file

The request also asked whether the Nuremberg holdings contain anything further on
Felix — as a witness in Case 11 (the Wilhelmstrasse trial) concerning the Dresdner
Bank, or in the NI/NID document series beyond NI/NID-6681, NID-7089 and NID-13829.
That question is left standing rather than repeated, so as not to complicate a
reply they have already begun to process.

Why this file matters: Felix was arrested in Vienna on 28 March 1945 on RSHA
orders, the allegations including a "Türkei-Feindverbindung" and connections to the
circle around General von Falkenhausen. The four 1947 interrogations touch both
points. We hold only the brief English Interrogation Summaries 4031, 4049, 4050 and
4108 from the Harvard Nuremberg Trials Project; these are the full German
transcripts.
