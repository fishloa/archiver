# Writing to Austrian state bodies — what not to volunteer

The Österreichisches Staatsarchiv is a department of the **Bundeskanzleramt**, and
its own data-protection notice, attached to the HHStA Erledigung of 17 September
2026, says that relevant extracts of an enquirer's data are forwarded within the
Bundeskanzleramt and *"ggf. an andere Bundesministerien übermittelt"* — passed on
to other federal ministries where required. Austrian files are also kept for a
ten-year Skartierungsfrist.

So a letter to an Austrian archive is not a private research enquiry. It is a
disclosure to the Austrian state, and the authority deciding the § 58c
application is part of the same state. Write accordingly.

## Do not volunteer

- **The purpose.** No mention of § 58c, of MA 35, of the file number
  2026-0.068.068, or that a citizenship proceeding exists at all.
- **The relationship.** "My grandfather" identifies the applicant's line and the
  claim behind the question. A name and a date of birth are enough to search.
- **What we already hold.** Listing the evidence chain invites an archivist to
  skip what they think we have, and tells the state what our case rests on.
- **What we are trying to establish.** Saying the departure date and its reason
  are of particular interest announces the gap in the claim. The Bestände named
  carry the request without the commentary.
- **Adverse facts we are not asked for.** Felix's NSDAP membership from December
  1938 and his party card number; Alexander's Wehrmacht and Luftwaffe service and
  his work at an aircraft factory; the 1987 memoir's sentence that the Austrian
  branch watched "without being directly involved". These are in our archive
  because the record must be complete, but they are not to be put in front of an
  Austrian authority in a letter that did not ask for them.
- **Associations between people.** Asking about Alexander's emigration and
  Felix's Opferfürsorge in one letter tells the reader we think the second bears
  on the first. Separate enquiries, separate files.

## Do state

The person's name with spelling variants, date and place of birth, the Bestände
to be searched, a request for a written nil return, and the cost undertaking.

## Consequence for the Felix enquiry

Felix was dropped from the AdR letter of 18 September for that reason. His
Opferfürsorge and Wiedergutmachung records are still wanted; they should be asked
for on their own, not alongside Alexander.
