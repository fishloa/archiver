### What changed and why (and it went out only once approved)

The letter already sent asked which members of the signatory families appear in
their databases. The Memorial cannot answer that: they hold prisoner cards and
have no list of who signed. So the names go **in the body of the email**, and the
letter states plainly what is being looked for: **relatives of signatories who
were imprisoned, deported or killed** — not the signatories themselves, who are
the easy part.

The list is also published in the scholarship of Dr Zdeněk Hazdra (ÚSTR), so the
Memorial can verify it independently rather than taking a private compilation on
trust.

Dates are from Jan Drocár's compilation (record 3497). Its layout sets names and
dates in separate columns, which is easy to mis-pair — that is how "Zikmund Schlik,
died 1942 aged 25" got into my earlier note. The man who died in 1942 aged 25 is
**Ondřej Schlik**.
